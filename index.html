How this is calculated

Each SIP instalment is invested at the start of the month. A lump sum is invested at the start of the month you choose and grows until the end of the period. Returns compound every month.

Annual rate ÷ 12 matches most online SIP calculators. Exact annual return treats your rate as a compound annual growth rate (CAGR), which gives slightly lower figures.

How this is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of EMIs.

Each month, interest is charged on the principal still owed and the rest of the EMI reduces the principal. Assumes a fixed rate with no prepayments or fees. A lender's schedule can differ by a few rupees because of rounding.
